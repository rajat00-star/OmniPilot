Frequently asked questions.

What is Mistral Vibe?

Vibe is Mistral's AI chat assistant and agent for work and code. It combines an AI chat assistant (Vibe for work, formerly Le Chat) and an AI coding agent (Vibe for code) in a single product for individuals, teams, and enterprises.

What happened to Le Chat?

Le Chat is now Vibe. All your conversations, settings, and plans carry over.

What can I do on the free plan?

Get quick answers, search the web, and handle simple tasks. Upgrade to Pro for agents, long-horizon tasks, and all-day coding.

Does Vibe support voice input?

Yes. Vibe supports voice input powered by Voxtral.

Can I connect Vibe to my existing tools?

Yes, Vibe integrates with 100+ tools including email, calendar, Slack, GitHub, Jira, and more through connectors and full MCP compatibility.

How do I get started with Vibe for code?

Install the CLI from your terminal, launch in VS Code, JetBrains or Zed via ACP, or launch coding tasks directly in Vibe with remote agents and teleport back to the CLI to continue.

Can I deploy Vibe on my own infrastructure?

Enterprise customers can deploy Vibe on-premises, in a private cloud, or on Mistral Cloud with full data residency.

Can I fine-tune Vibe models on my data?

Fine-tuning is available for enterprise deployments through Mistral Forge.
