Think, create, and move work forward with Copilot
Home is your starting point in Copilot, bringing Chat and Cowork together in one place. Review recent activity, discover suggested actions, pick up where you left off, or start something new. Use Chat for answers grounded in your work, or hand off complex, multi-step work to Cowork across your apps, data, and workflows. Home view is available in Microsoft Frontier.

AI that knows your work
AI integrated into your apps

Copilot is integrated into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams, where your work already happens.

Grounded in your work

Work IQ grounds Copilot in your company, your team’s roles, and the context behind your projects.

Leading AI models, all in one place

Copilot brings together leading AI models with Work IQ so you can benefit from the distinct strengths of each in a single experience.

Auto advantage, the right model for the task

Focus on the work, not the model. Auto weighs accuracy, speed, and cost for each request, helping determine the right model and effort for the task at hand. Looking ahead, Copilot will also help orchestrate work across Chat, Cowork, and Code.

Build your agents. Work your way.
Create agents for work

Turn your expertise into personalized, work-grounded agents in minutes using natural language with Agent Builder in Copilot. Extend and manage them with Copilot Studio when you're ready to scale.

One Copilot across your devices

Work with Copilot across desktop, mobile, and web using voice, keyboard, or pen.

Keeps your work secure
Copilot is designed to be secure and private, built on the trusted Microsoft 365 foundation your organization already uses. Enterprise-grade controls protect your data, people, and business, while keeping work and personal accounts separate.
